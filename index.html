About

I am a PhD candidate in Statistics in the Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science at the University of Waterloo, supervised by Professor Ali Ghodsi. I am also a Faculty Affiliate Researcher at the Vector Institute. My research interests include Bayesian deep learning, variational inference, machine learning theory, uncertainty quantification, and efficient language models.

In the Data Analytics Laboratory, I study how variational Bayesian neural networks scale with model width and how Transformers can adapt their width across depth. You can read about my current projects and publications on the research page.

I began my PhD in January 2025, with expected completion in 2028. Previously, I completed a Master of Mathematics in Statistics (2023–2024), supervised by Professor Ali Ghodsi, and an Honours Bachelor of Mathematics in Statistics with a Minor in Computing through the Co-operative Program (2018–2023), both at Waterloo.

Selected experience

  • Intact Financial Corporation — Data Scientist I, Usage-Based Insurance (June 2023–December 2024), following data science internships in 2021 and 2022. I worked on telematics, driving-risk modelling, and production driving-event logic.
  • TD Bank — Data Science and Advanced Analytics Intern (May–August 2022). I developed SQL and PySpark pipelines, automated analytics workflows, and contributed to Azure migration work for TD Wealth.
  • National Research Council Canada — Research Assistant (January–April 2021). I developed a transfer-learning framework for COVID-19 patient-severity prediction using influenza and COVID-19 clinical data.

Teaching

At Waterloo, I served as Head Teaching Assistant in 2024 and 2026 for STAT 940 (Deep Learning) and STAT 442/842 (Data Visualization). My teaching assistant experience from 2023 to 2026 spans statistical learning, advanced regression, case studies in data science, computational statistics, forecasting, data visualization, and introductory statistics.
